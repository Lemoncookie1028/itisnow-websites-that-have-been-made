Rules

  • No griefing. Destroying or altering other players' builds without permission is strictly prohibited.
  • No hacking or cheating. X-ray, fly hacks, auto-clickers, or any unfair modifications aren't allowed - this includes exploits like duping.
  • Be respectful. Treat others with kindness. Harassment, hate speech, or toxic behavior will not be tolerated.
  • No being homophobic, racist, etc. Self explanatory, no exceptions.
  • No stealing. Don't take items from other players' chests or property unless explicitly allowed.
  • Keep chat clean. Avoid spamming, excessive swearing, or inappropriate content. Keep it friendly.
  • No advertising. Don't promote other servers or external content without permission - ask first.
  • Changing usernames. Update your username in the Discord if you change it in-game. If you get banned over a mismatched name, hop on with an account that matches or update it in Discord and reach out - you'll get unbanned.
  • Use common sense. Don't be a jerk. If you think something might be against the rules, it probably is - just ask if you're unsure.

By playing on this server, you agree to follow these rules. Thanks, and have fun :)
